Who We Are

The ACM Student Chapter at Sinai University is a community of tech-driven students who love learning, building, and innovating.

We aim to create an environment that supports growth in programming, AI, software development, and all fields of computing.

Through workshops, sessions with industry experts, competitions, and training opportunities, we help students develop real skills and explore new opportunities.

Our mission is to advance computing, connect students with professionals, and inspire collaboration that makes a real impact.
